SPAL's proprietary geometric marks, use instead of emoji, stock icons or mascots wherever a concept needs a visual anchor (empty states, loading states, onboarding, dividers, coach cards).

```jsx
<SpalSymbol symbol="cashflow" color="var(--spal-green)" size={48} />
```

8 symbols: `profit` (expanding rings), `flow`/`loop` (orbital motion), `growth` (ascending layers), `focus` (concentric circles), `consistency` (repeating grid), `momentum` (directional chevron), `insight` (radiating structure), `cashflow` (continuous wave). `spin` animates a slow continuous rotation for flow/loop contexts.
